## Gap Summary

- C1 can be claimed now only as a bounded systems contribution: a
  research-operations and measurement substrate for existing multi-file
  repositories and competing published methods.
- C2 should be the main empirical contribution, but its result must remain
  neutral until the corrected campaign and certification analysis finish.
- C1 and C2 should be presented as a dependency rather than two unrelated
  novelties: C1 supplies the intervention and evidence contract that makes C2
  measurable.
- C3 remains unassigned. Rank elasticity is currently an analysis inside C2,
  not an independent contribution.
- C4 and C5 should appear as future or conditional contributions only if their
  promotion gates are later met.
- Blocked statements include ranking flips or stability, elimination of recipe
  bias, cross-cohort robustness, rank elasticity, matched-success fairness, and
  production-equivalent Local/SLURM/Ray behavior.
