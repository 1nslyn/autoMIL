# Confirmed Contribution

## Core Contribution

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Main contribution statement | We introduce autoMIL, a research-operations system that represents coding-agent interventions as parent-addressed source overlays, reconstructs them in isolated worktrees, and mediates declared file permissions, attempt budgets, validation-only selection, and separate held-out certification. Its pathology benchmark consumer adds a predeclared method-identity and programmatic-recipe contract so competing published methods can be studied under an auditable research-opportunity protocol. |
| Contribution type | New system. The second contribution is a new analysis/benchmark enabled by the system: a controlled pathology-MIL ranking audit under native no-search and matched-search regimes. |
| One-sentence reviewer payoff | autoMIL turns otherwise incomparable source-level research loops into reconstructable evidence, making it possible to ask whether a method ranking reflects the method or the unequal research effort spent on it. |

## Why This Contribution Is Needed

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Field problem | Published machine-learning rankings combine method quality with heterogeneous human choices about recipes, tuning, debugging, and source-level modifications, while coding agents make this latent research-effort variable larger rather than automatically controlled. |
| Specific gap | The field lacks an executable, repository-level protocol for assigning competing existing methods a matched declared opportunity for autonomous source-level research while retaining attributable method identity, a guarded programmatic train-only recipe surface, failure-inclusive effort accounting, validation-only selection, and separately revealed held-out evidence. |
| Concrete challenge | A source-level agent can edit multiple files, silently reduce a method to another through flags or loss weights, crash or consume partial budgets, adapt decisions to validation results, and accidentally obtain final-test information. A valid comparison must allow more than a finite scalar menu while preventing edits that replace the method being attributed. |
| Why prior work leaves it unresolved | AIDE, AIRA/AIRA2, AI Scientist-v2, MLAgentBench, MLE-bench, MLE-Dojo, RE-Bench, MLRC-Bench, and AMID already establish code editing, candidate graphs, isolated execution, budgets, trajectories, and hidden evaluation. They primarily evaluate agents or optimize solutions. PathBench-MIL and nnMIL provide pathology-MIL infrastructure and configured pipelines. None of the closest work identified here directly instantiates the combined repository-level contract to estimate how a published pathology-MIL cross-method ranking responds when every method receives the same declared, attributable autonomous source-level research opportunity. |

## How This Paper Responds

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Design response | autoMIL separates a research layer from an execution layer and an evidence layer. It stores each candidate as a parent-linked overlay; reconstructs it in a git worktree; declares editable and protected surfaces; assigns a per-cell launched-attempt and time budget; records trajectories, failures, and usable results; permits selection only from validation metrics; and quarantines held-out metrics for a separate certification path. Autobench additionally declares each arm's method identity, freezes defining inference/forward/core-loss mechanisms, permits scalar and executable train-only recipe changes, and classifies candidates before the cross-method comparison. |
| Evidence required | Code and invariant tests for reconstruction, permissions, accounting, recovery, result splitting, certification, and per-arm method identity; adversarial non-interference tests; a runnable programmatic-recipe channel and archived candidate classification; real end-to-end executions with attributable overlays and failure semantics; exact attempt, completion, compute, time, and LLM-cost ledgers; a frozen native/no-search cross-arm leaderboard; a matched-search cross-arm leaderboard containing only identity-preserving candidates; within-method lift, rank shift or stability, and anytime curves; seed-aware and cohort-dependence-aware uncertainty; explicit TITAN treatment; and reproducible artifact release. |
| Evidence available | The repository implements the experiment graph, parent overlays, worktree runner, registry, budget cells, result schema, certification path, trajectory capture/redaction, recovery, local/SLURM/Ray interfaces, and framework-purity separation, with extensive unit and integration tests. Autobench contains the five-cohort roster, crossed encoder and tile-aggregator design, a separate slide-level TITAN regime, per-arm provenance, validation-only screening, static-grid artifacts, and explicit contribution/audit ledgers. |
| Evidence missing | The corrected native baseline must be regenerated and reconciled against the canonical roster; OPT-1 and provenance wording remain open; the method-identity policy is settled but its per-arm enforcement and runnable programmatic-recipe channel are not implemented; the full matched autonomous campaign and sealed-test ranking analysis are incomplete; multi-seed evidence is currently deferred; shared-cohort dependence needs a valid inferential design; production semantics across real SLURM and Ray have not established backend equivalence; and the strongest domain-generality claim lacks another non-toy consumer. |

## Claim Boundary

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Strong claims allowed | autoMIL implements a repository-level operations and evidence contract comprising parent-addressed source overlays, worktree reconstruction, declared file permissions, failure-inclusive launched-attempt caps with usable-result reporting, validation-only search signals, and a distinct held-out certification path. The current paper can describe the preregistered pathology-MIL audit design and the verified static roster without asserting its unfinished outcome. |
| Claims to soften or avoid | Do not claim that autoMIL invents code-space search, experiment trees, budgets, sandboxing, hidden tests, provenance, or autonomous scientific discovery. Do not yet claim that pathology-MIL rankings change or remain stable, that recipe bias is eliminated, that the 165 cells are independent, that equal attempts imply equal compute or search difficulty, that sealing is an OS security boundary, that Local/SLURM/Ray are production-equivalent, or that arbitrary projects require zero adaptation. |
| Novelty risk | A reviewer may call autoMIL an integration of mechanisms already present in AIDE, AIRA2, MLRC-Bench, and AMID. The honest answer is that the individual mechanisms are not claimed as new; the contribution is their executable organization as a method-aware measurement instrument for an estimand those systems do not target, with C2 providing the scientific test of that measurement capability. |
| Significance risk | A reviewer may view one pathology benchmark as too narrow or the framework as unnecessary if rankings do not change. The answer is that either outcome is informative under the controlled protocol: instability quantifies research-effort sensitivity, while stability validates the benchmark against that confound. Significance nevertheless depends on completing the campaign, publishing the evidence ledger, and limiting generality beyond the evaluated consumers and infrastructure. |
